What is PinkOut?

A women-first lifestyle, entertainment and community destination launching in Riyadh.

Where is PinkOut?

Riyadh. The exact location will be announced soon.

When will PinkOut open?

Opening details will be announced through the app and waitlist. An opening date has not been confirmed yet.

How much is entry?

General entry is planned at SAR 35. Tickets are not on sale yet.

Is everything inside free?

Entry includes selected free activities. Some experiences, services and classes will have additional fees through the app.

Do I need the app?

The PinkOut app is the main way to discover schedules, experiences, reservations and updates, and stay connected after the event.

Who is PinkOut for?

PinkOut is created primarily for women. Full guest and companion policies will be announced before ticket sales open.

Can children attend?

Final age and child-access policies will be announced before opening.

Can accompanying men attend?

Companion access rules and dedicated facilities will be confirmed before opening.

Can my brand partner with PinkOut?

Yes. Explore the partnership opportunities and use the enquiry form when applications open.

Privacy

The official privacy policy is being prepared and will be published before data collection begins. In this version, registration and enquiry forms are not active; details you enter are neither submitted nor saved.

Only your language choice is saved on your device. The Alexandria typeface loads from this website without requests to external services.

Terms

Official terms are being prepared and will be published before tickets go on sale. Current information and imagery introduce the upcoming concept. The location, opening date, age and companion policies are not confirmed yet.

General entry is planned at SAR 35. This website does not sell tickets or accept payments; transactions will be available through the PinkOut app.

Accessibility

Navigate using your keyboard, or jump to the content with the skip link. Forms have visible labels and both Arabic and English are supported. Close any dialog with its close button or Escape.

The site respects your device’s reduced-motion preference. You can enlarge text in your browser and read the site without JavaScript; additional destination details and forms need JavaScript.
