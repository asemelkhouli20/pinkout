# PinkOut photographic conversion notes

The original designs are retained in the approved daylight photographic direction. Built-in Create Image was used for 14 venue scenes and one live-action interpretation of the original animated K-pop trio. Seven original real themed-day photographs were restored without generative retouching. App mockups remain illustrative. The venue images remain concepts, not photographs of an existing PinkOut venue.

## Hero direction approved by the user

Rebuild the original PinkOut facade as a candid real-world event-installation photograph under soft overcast late-afternoon daylight. Keep the original architecture, signage, green facade, woman-shaped sign, draped fabric and floral placements. Dry matte slightly dusty road/paving, realistic painted metal seams, irregular fabric creases, natural cut flowers, ordinary glass reflections, candid visitors and restrained practical lights. No cinematic glow, HDR, plastic surfaces or pink/orange color wash. The user approved this daylight version as the direction for the remaining scenes.

## Shared editing prompt

Image 1 is the ORIGINAL PinkOut scene to edit. Image 2 is the APPROVED photographic-realism reference (the daylight facade), only for the natural camera look, materials and lighting—not for its architecture or objects. Reconstruct the scene in image 1 as a believable documentary photograph of a physically built venue. This is a MAJOR photographic conversion, not sharpening, beauty retouching or preserving its CGI surface quality. Keep the recognizable original architecture, camera viewpoint, spatial layout, pink/ivory/green palette, key fixtures, equipment, objects and depicted activity. Replace the artificial appearance throughout. Neutral soft daylight from the existing openings plus ordinary warm-white practical fixtures. Pink is paint/fabric, not colored illumination covering every surface. Subdued LEDs with controlled highlights, matte slightly worn paint, real wood grain, stone joints, brushed metal, slight upholstery creases, irregular flower petals and leaves, weak physically consistent glass reflections, believable contact shadows. Realistic people with natural skin, asymmetrical candid expressions, individual hair strands, ordinary anatomy and unposed body language; retain clothing and approximate positions. Printed signs and displays have actual physical thickness, sensible brightness and the original branding, not luminous floating graphics. The photographic reference's restrained exposure, imperfect white balance, subtle grain, realistic depth and dry/matte materials. No HDR, cinematic pink/orange wash, neon glow, glossy plastic skin, showroom perfection, luminous flowers, over-smoothing, aggressive bokeh, ray-traced perfection or fantasy atmosphere. No alternate location or generic stock substitute. One image with original framing/aspect ratio.
